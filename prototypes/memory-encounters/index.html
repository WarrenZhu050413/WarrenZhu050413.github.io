Three small encounters

One small action that the eye can understand: notice, approach, pause, part. The mouse has a tapered nose, a low body, fine paws and a trailing tail. The illustrations remain soft silhouettes; the movement carries the relationship.

A low flight ↗

The butterfly dips toward the mouse. It stops, leans up, then takes a few steps after it. The most literal and legible encounter.

Shadows on a page ↗

The butterfly passes through the poem. Its shadow reaches the mouse first, bringing the encounter into the writing.

Two sides of the glass ↗

They approach one another but remain on opposite sides. The glass becomes part of the encounter.
