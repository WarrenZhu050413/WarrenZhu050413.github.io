I wish it were not dark.

  1. Commonplace

    Text only. Words clear at the centre of the page and quieten at the edges. Click a line to keep it: it stays inked, a copy goes into the margin, and the kept lines become the last page.

  2. Window

    One scene held behind old glass through the whole essay. Each passage brings its part of the scene forward, and wiped glass stays clear.

  3. Watermark

    The images are in the paper. Hold a passage up to the light to see its watermark, and click to keep it held.

  4. Lamplight

    Dusk falls down the essay. Light the lamp each text names; lit lamps stay lit and follow you down the page.

  5. Fixer

    Each print develops as you read it and fogs once you have passed. Press and hold to fix a print so it stays.
